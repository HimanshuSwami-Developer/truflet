What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Profile: name, mobile number, address, handle and photo
  • Your sports and skill levels
  • Saved location, city and area
  • Saved venues, reviews and notifications
  • Push notification tokens for your devices
  • Your wallet balance and referral code

What we keep

  • Booking, payment and refund records, kept only as long as tax and accounting laws require (up to 8 years), then deleted.
  • Messages in match chats are anonymised, so other players see “Deleted user” instead of your name.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security, then deleted.

Deleting your account can’t be undone. Any unused wallet balance is lost. Read our Privacy Policy for more.
